About

Electrical engineering at Columbia University, class of 2029. Since 15, I've explored the intersection of hardware and software — specializing in embedded systems, analog and digital circuit design, and PCB layout. Most of what I know came from mixed-signal boards that didn't work the first time.

Beyond reading datasheets and applying Ohm's law, I've built up an intuitive understanding of circuits from years of hands-on tinkering. I pride myself on a genuine passion for electrical engineering and the satisfaction of bringing ideas to life through circuit design.

KiCad · Altium · LTspice · Arduino · embedded C · SPI, I²C · Python · MATLAB · scope & spectrum analyzers
